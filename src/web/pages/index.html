Alt text for every slide, written for you

cappv1 describes the pictures, charts and tables in your PowerPoint files, so people who use screen readers aren't left with silence. Your original files are never changed.

Three steps

  1. Add your decksDrop in .pptx files. Your originals stay where they are.
  2. Choose who describes themA model on your own computer, or a cloud service you have a key for.
  3. Download the finished copiesSame slides, now with alt text. Review them before you share.

You decide where your slides go

Stay private

Use Ollama and everything happens on this computer. Pictures and slide text never leave it, and it works offline once a model is downloaded.

Use a cloud service

OpenRouter, OpenAI and Anthropic are faster and often better, but the pictures and text they describe are sent to them. cappv1 always says which one is active.

Read exactly what is sent and what is kept

Careful with your files

  • It writes alt text, and marks a table's top row as its header when that changes nothing you can see. Layout, text, colors, pictures and slide order come through exactly as they were.
  • If someone already wrote alt text, cappv1 keeps it or improves it. It doesn't overwrite it unless you ask.
  • If a run stops, nothing is lost. Run it again and it carries on, and slides already described cost nothing.
  • AI can get things wrong. Read the results, especially for important or public decks.
